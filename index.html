The Problem

AI should not require
giving up control.

Modern AI workflows often depend on external cloud services. For sensitive environments, this creates serious privacy, security and operational challenges.

Cloud Dependency

AI workflows often require external APIs and continuous internet connectivity.

Data Privacy

Sensitive documents and organizational knowledge may leave the controlled environment.

Vendor Lock-in

Dependence on proprietary AI providers limits control over models and infrastructure.

Knowledge Silos

Documents, organizational knowledge and AI tools are often disconnected.

Resource Constraints

Deploying capable AI systems locally can be difficult on limited hardware.

Fragmented Workflows

Research, coding, retrieval and document generation often require multiple disconnected tools.

The Solution

One workbench.
Complete control.

Swanirbhar brings local AI models, agentic orchestration, knowledge retrieval and secure execution into one unified workspace.

Local-first AI

Run open-weight AI models directly within your infrastructure.

Agentic orchestration

Complex requests are decomposed into specialized tasks automatically.

Hybrid knowledge retrieval

Combine keyword and semantic search for better document grounding.

Secure execution

Coding and execution tasks can run inside an isolated environment.
